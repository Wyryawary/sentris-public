    • block
      A single AI‑generated text unit (word or short phrase) that the player “drops” into one of the board’s columns. Players stack blocks to form sentences in each row.

    • active block
      The block currently in motion/fall—i.e. the one the player is positioning left/right or swapping text content on before it settles into place.

    • locked row
      A horizontal row whose assembled sentence has been judged invalid by the AI backend. Locked rows cannot accept new (replacement) blocks, only during bonus rounds.

    • bonus round
      A special phase triggered when a row is cleared. During a bonus round, the player may replace blocks in locked rows and resubmit them for re‑validation in an attempt to clear the lock.

    • overlay panel
      Any semi‑transparent UI layer that floats over the main game board—used for notifications, menus, prompts or status messages without leaving the current screen.

    • custom topics
      Optional user‑provided keywords or themes sent to the AI generator to bias the content of new blocks (e.g. “space,” “mystery,” “humor”).
